# Capstone 2026-2027, Maria Dumitrescu

Minerva University, M27. Advisor: Prof Gloria Tam.
Independent, self-designed track, weighted to Computational Sciences.

This repository is the readable half of my capstone. It holds the documents, the decision
log, and the reasoning. The two working repositories stay private, because one of them
holds credentials and the other is a live app with real users' private journals in it.
What a reader needs in order to follow the project is here.

## The project in one sentence

A vertically integrated consumer venture in human connection: an audience engine that
delivered 1,715,696 views in the 90 days to 21 September 2026, and Nuuko, a journaling app
it feeds people into. What I am testing is whether owning both halves turns attention into
retained users at a rate a normal app cannot buy.

## Where to start

**[PROJECT-BRIEF.md](PROJECT-BRIEF.md)** is the week 3 brief and the place to begin. Scope,
the line between prior work and capstone work drawn with dates, the success measures with
their baselines, the rubrics written before the data, and the full HC and LO list.

**[CAPSTONE-PLAN.md](CAPSTONE-PLAN.md)** is the map of the year: deadlines, the outcome
arithmetic, the risks, what is built and what is not.

**[DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md)** is the decision log, newest first, one entry per decision
in a fixed shape. A decision I reverse is never deleted. It gets marked superseded and the
newer entry links back to it. This is the spine of the process documentation.

**[NUUKO-STRATEGY.md](NUUKO-STRATEGY.md)** is the product direction for the app.
**[RESEARCH-SYNTHESIS.md](RESEARCH-SYNTHESIS.md)** is the literature and user research
underneath it. **[LANES.md](LANES.md)** says which work goes to which tool and what no tool
is allowed to do alone, which is my honest answer to the AI question.

## What is not here, and why

The two code repositories. A snapshot of a repository that is still moving misleads a week
later, and both of these move daily. I would rather walk a reader through the live code in
a meeting than hand over a frozen copy.

The parts of the decision log that are not capstone work. The full log also covers writing
and publishing work that Part 1 of the brief puts out of scope. Those entries are not here,
which is why the dates skip.

Anything that would be unsafe in public: credentials, third-party contacts, and details of
weaknesses in a live app that holds other people's private writing.
